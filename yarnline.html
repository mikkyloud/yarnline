On the name: a "yarn" is what people call the talk, the gist, the story someone's telling — and a line is what carries a voice from one person to the next. Yarnline just gives that line a stop in every language. Speech recognition and playback here depend on your browser — Chrome handles Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, and Pidgin reasonably well; for less common languages, typing is the safer route. Translations come from AI and are a strong starting point, not a certified translation.
